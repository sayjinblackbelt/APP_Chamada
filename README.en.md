# 📋 Attendance Management App

[🇧🇷 Português](README.md) | 🇺🇸 English | [🇪🇸 Español](README.es.md)

> An application under development to make attendance tracking for workshops, classes, and educational activities simpler, faster, and more organized.

## 📌 Project origin

The idea emerged from a **real institutional demand identified in 2025**, related to improving and simplifying attendance registration in educational activities.

In **2026**, the project moved from an identified operational need to the beginning of technical development and MVP structuring.

The goal is to transform a real operational demand into a practical and progressively evolving digital solution without publicly associating the project with a specific institution.

## 🎯 Objective

Replace or simplify manual attendance processes, allowing educators and responsible teams to register attendance from a phone or tablet with only a few taps.

The solution aims to reduce:

- time spent on manual records;
- rework;
- inconsistencies;
- difficulty accessing attendance history.

## 📱 MVP

- activity, workshop, or class selection;
- participant list;
- present or absent registration;
- automatic date and time recording;
- corrections before confirmation;
- attendance summary;
- basic attendance history.

## 🧠 Project principles

1. **Simplicity over unnecessary features.**
2. Mobile and tablet-friendly interface.
3. Fast operation during in-person activities.
4. Architecture prepared for unstable connectivity and future evolution.
5. Privacy and data protection from the beginning.
6. Clear separation between demonstration and real data.
7. Documentation as a reference for technical decisions.

## 🔒 Privacy

Attendance applications may process personal data. Public repositories must not contain real participant data, real attendance lists, credentials, tokens, or API keys.

Public examples should use fictional or anonymized data.
